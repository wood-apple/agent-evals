On 9 November 2026 between 14:02 and 14:44 UTC (about 42 minutes), roughly a third of requests from our mobile apps and API failed, while the web app was largely unaffected. The cause was an expired security certificate that our automatic renewal process had failed to replace. We deployed a new certificate at 14:44 UTC and service has been fully restored. To prevent this from happening again we are fixing the renewal automation and adding alerts well ahead of any certificate expiry. We're sorry for the disruption this caused.
